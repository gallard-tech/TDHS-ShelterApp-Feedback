# 🐾 TDHS Shelter App Feedback Portal

Welcome to the feedback portal for the **Timmins & District Humane Society Shelter Management System**!

We're developing a new application to help TDHS staff and volunteers manage animals, medical records, shelter operations, adoptions, and more.

Your ideas and feedback will help shape the system as it develops.

---

## 💡 Share Your Feedback

Whether you've found a problem, have an idea for a new feature, or simply think something could work better, we'd love to hear from you!

Have an idea for something the application should do?

[**Submit a Feature Request**](../../discussions/categories/feature-requests)

## 👍 Support Existing Ideas

Before submitting something new, check whether another staff member has already suggested it.

You can:

- Upvote existing suggestions
- Add comments
- Share additional examples

This helps us identify which improvements matter most.

---

## 🔄 What Happens Next?

1. You submit feedback.
2. The development team reviews it.
3. Additional information may be requested.
4. Approved suggestions may be added to the development roadmap.
5. Improvements are developed and tested.
6. Updates are shared when available.

Submitting an idea doesn't guarantee it will be implemented, but every constructive suggestion is appreciated.

---

## 🔒 Privacy and Confidentiality

**This feedback portal is public.**

Please do not include:

- Passwords or login credentials
- Personal information about adopters, owners, or staff
- Confidential animal medical information
- Private shelter records
- Screenshots containing sensitive information

Use fictional examples or remove identifying information when describing problems.

If an issue involves sensitive information, report it directly to a TDHS administrator instead.
